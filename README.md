# Business Data Exploration | MySQL (Star Schema & Exploratory Data Analysis)

An end-to-end SQL project exploring business data to understand sales performance, customer behaviour and product contribution. The focus was on using SQL to answer practical business questions and turn raw data into meaningful insights.

## Project Overview

Built a relational database using a Star Schema consisting of customer, product and sales tables. Used MySQL to explore the data, calculate business metrics and analyse sales patterns across different dimensions.

## What I Analysed

- **Customer Insights:** Customer distribution by country, gender and marital status.
- **Product Performance:** Product category contribution, best-selling products and sales distribution.
- **Sales Performance:** Revenue, quantity sold, order volume and average selling price.
- **Customer Performance:** Revenue contribution and order activity across customers.
- **Profitability:** Total profit, profit percentage and average cost and selling price across product categories.

## SQL Skills Applied

Database creation, table design, Star Schema, data loading, primary and foreign key constraints, data exploration, aggregate functions, JOINs, subqueries, GROUP BY, ORDER BY, UNION ALL and ranking queries.

## Key Findings

- Bikes contributed approximately 96% of total sales, making them the dominant product category.
- The United States and Australia together accounted for more than 50% of total sales.
- The customer base was relatively balanced by gender, with a slight male majority (51%).
- The business recorded approximately 27.7K orders from 18.5K customers.

## Business KPI Report

Generated a consolidated SQL report covering key business metrics, including revenue, profitability, customer base, product count and order volume.
